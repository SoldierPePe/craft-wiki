## 1. Install LightCraft

The quickest way is [ArtCraft Launcher](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/releases/latest): install it, then press **Install** on LightCraft's card. To download LightCraft on its own, see [Install and update](/apps/lightcraft/install-update/).

## 2. Open LightCraft

LightCraft opens your library, a folder at `Pictures/LightCraft Library` in your home folder. A new library starts with a few demo photos, so you can try things out right away.

The sidebar on the left lists **All Photos**, **Recently Added**, **Picks**, your **Albums**, **By Date** and **Recently Deleted**.

![The Photo Grid showing All Photos from the demo library, with ratings and flags on the thumbnails. The sidebar on the left lists Recently Added, Picks, albums inside a Travel 2026 folder, By Date and Recently Deleted, and the Edit panel is open on the right](/assets/uploads/lightcraft/grid.webp)

## 3. Import your photos

1. Choose **File › Import Photos…** to pick files, or **File › Import from Folder…** to bring in a whole folder.
2. In the **Import Photos** window, check the photos you want. Photos already in your library start unchecked.
3. Under **Transfer**, choose **Add in place** to leave the files where they are, or **Copy** to copy them into the library.
4. Click the import button at the bottom.

The import runs in the background, so you can carry on working. For more options, see [Workflows](/apps/lightcraft/workflows/#import-photos).

## 4. Rate and flag

Select a photo and press a key:

- **1** to **5** sets the star rating, and **0** clears it.
- **P** flags it as a pick, **X** as rejected, and **U** clears the flag.

Turn on **Photo › Auto Advance** to move on to the next photo after each key.

## 5. Edit

1. Double-click a photo to open it in the **Detail View**.
2. Press **E** to open the **Edit** panel.
3. Click **Auto** for a quick start, then adjust the sliders in **Light**, **Color** and **Effects**.
4. Press **\\** to switch between your edit and the original.

Every change can be undone with **Edit › Undo** (**Ctrl+Z**, or **⌘Z** on a Mac). **Reset** at the top of the **Edit** panel removes all edits from the photo. Your original file is never changed.

## 6. Export

1. Select the photos in the grid.
2. Choose **File › Export…**.
3. Pick a **Format**, such as **JPEG**, and a size.
4. Next to **Folder**, click **Choose…** and pick where the files go.
5. Click the export button at the bottom of the window.

You don't need to save: LightCraft saves your library as you work.
