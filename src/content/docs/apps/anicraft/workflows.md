On macOS, substitute **⌘** for **Ctrl** in the shortcuts below.

## Interface overview

| Panel          | Purpose                                                                |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Stage**      | The central canvas where your vector artwork is drawn and viewed.       |
| **Timeline**   | Layers, keyframes, frame ranges, onion skinning, and playback controls. |
| **Tools**      | Selection, Subselection, Free Transform, Pen, Pencil, Shapes, and Fills.|
| **Properties** | Object dimensions, fill/stroke colors, tween easing, and stage settings.|
| **Library**    | Graphic symbols, movie clips, bitmap assets, and imported media.       |

## Command palette

Press **Ctrl+K** (or **⌘K** on macOS) at any time to open the **Command Palette**. Search for any tool, menu item, or operation and execute it with instant keyboard navigation.

![AniCraft's command palette searching for keyframe operations](/assets/uploads/anicraft/palette.webp)

## Symbols and nested timelines

Turn reusable artwork into symbols:

1. Select artwork on the stage and press **F8** (**Modify › Convert to Symbol...**).
2. Choose **Graphic** or **Movie Clip**.
3. Double-click the instance to edit its nested internal timeline.
4. Use the **Free Transform** tool to scale, rotate, skew, or position the instance.

![Using Free Transform on a symbol instance on the stage](/assets/uploads/anicraft/transform.webp)

## Onion skinning

Click the **Onion Skin** toggle on the timeline header to view semi-transparent previews of preceding and succeeding frames while in-betweening hand-drawn motion.
